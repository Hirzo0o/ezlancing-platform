# Ezlancing BRD — draft v0.1
## Vision
Original bilingual freelance marketplace for project-based hiring, packaged services and talent discovery.
## Actors
Visitor, client, freelancer, administrator, support/moderator. Dual client/freelancer capabilities require design.
## MVP flows (planned)
Registration; freelancer/client profiles; projects; proposals; invitations; service offers; messaging; agreements; reviews; moderation.
## Out of scope for Sprint 0
Real authentication, actual transactions, escrow, payouts, production deployment, and completed Arabic localization.
## Open business decisions
Launch jurisdictions and currencies, commission structure, identity checks, payment partner eligibility, dispute rules and legal counsel review.
