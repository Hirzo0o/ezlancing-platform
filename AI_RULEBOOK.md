# Ezlancing AI Rulebook
1. Inspect existing work and plan first. Never overwrite unrelated files or repos.
2. Preserve official brand tokens and approved logo assets; do not invent replacements.
3. Work on one requested feature at a time; document assumptions.
4. Do not install additional dependencies, change the stack, merge, or deploy without approval.
5. Protect secrets, user data, and ownership boundaries. Authorization is server-side.
6. Payments, payouts and escrow remain disabled until provider/legal review.
7. Test and build before declaring success; report actual outcomes.
8. Prefer the term 'agreement' for platform legal work.
